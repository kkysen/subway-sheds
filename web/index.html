NYC Travel Time Map with QueensLink

Explore how long it takes to get anywhere in NYC by subway and walking, starting from any station, and how much sooner proposed changes to the subway, so far just QueensLink, would get you there. New stations are shown with . and how much sooner QueensLink would get you there. New QueensLink stations are shown with . The travel time includes entering the starting station, waiting for trains, transferring, exiting the final station, and walking to the destination.

How to use it

ClickTap a station to start. The map then colors the city in isochrones, showing how many minutes it takes to get everywhere from there. If you hoverlong press anywhere, it will show you the route there, how long it takes, and how long each step of the journey takes. The darker the orange, the longer it takes to get there. The darker the blue, the more time you save with QueensLink.

  • When lets you choose between Weekday @ 8 am or Saturday @ 12 pm (assuming no weekend closures).
  • Minutes lets you switch between showing the time with or without each change (toggle its logo, like for QueensLink) or show the time saved with them, as well as the route with and route without them.
  • Travel Time lets you switch between showing the travel time Without QueensLink, the time With it, or the time Saved with QueensLink, as well as the route with and route without QueensLink.
  • Walking lets you set the walking speed from the final station to the destination. Average walking speed is 3 mph.
  • $3 and $∞ let you choose between a single- and a multi-fare ride, assuming you've hit your fare cap.
  • Minutes from Station shows the isochrone legend. Hovering overLong pressing each color band in the legend highlights it on the map, and vice versa.

QueensLink

Route

QueensLink extends the M from 63 Dr-Rego Park, down the abandoned Rockaway Beach Branch, and along the A to the Rockaways (on this map, to Rockaway Park), replacing the H. The M stops serving Forest Hills, but on weekdays the G is extended from Court Sq to Forest Hills.

Schedule

The QueensLink GTFS schedule was built by adding the QueensLink changes to the current MTA schedule (current except for the full 7X being restored). On weekdays during the day, the current Ms keep their times. At other times, the M is extended to its full route, as frequent as the F. Run times on the new track are simulated from its curves, with trains accelerating and braking as R188s do under CBTC on the 7, and padded like the Queens Blvd local, which also runs under CBTC. Then the schedule is adjusted slightly so every train stays safely spaced: the Ms and the trains they share track with run a little earlier or later, and hold a little longer or shorter at a few stations, as optimized by a constraint satisfiability (CP-SAT) solver. The G is extended to Forest Hills on weekdays. More details are in the README.
