NYC Travel Times

How long it takes to get anywhere in New York City by subway, from any station, and how much sooner QueensLink would get you there.

What it shows

Pick a station, and the map colors the city by how many minutes it takes to get everywhere from there: riding trains, changing between them, and walking the streets the rest of the way.

Waiting for trains counts too. Each time is the median trip leaving every minute of an hour, 7:30 to 8:30 am on a weekday or 11:30 am to 12:30 pm on a Saturday, on the subway's own schedules, as for someone who doesn't time when they leave.

How to use it

  • Click or tap a station to see travel times from it.
  • Point at a place, or tap it on a phone, to see the quickest trip there. Point at a station, or hold it on a phone, to see the trip to it.
  • When picks a weekday morning or a Saturday midday.
  • Time Saved shows how many minutes QueensLink saves, rather than how many it takes.
  • Walking sets how fast you walk. $3 is one fare; $∞ also changes trains outside the system, paying again.

QueensLink

QueensLink would run the M over the unused Rockaway Beach Branch: off the Queens Blvd line at 63 Dr-Rego Park, through new stations at Metropolitan Av-Parkside, Jamaica Av, Atlantic Av-Woodhaven, and Liberty Av, then along the A to Broad Channel and Rockaway Park. On weekdays, the G would run on to Forest Hills in its place.

Its new stations are drawn as M bullets. Turn it on or off with its logo by Time, or pick Time Saved to see what it changes. How it's scheduled is in the code's README.
